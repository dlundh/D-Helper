CPU temperature limit

The CPU slows itself down at this temperature. Fans max out 3°C before it.

--
Range 75–100°C (the chip's own maximum is 105°C). Applies within a second, even mid-game.

Steady clocks

When the CPU keeps hitting its limit even with max fans, D-Helper lowers its power step by step until it sits just under the limit: a slightly lower but steady clock instead of boost-and-crash stutter. Power comes back when there's headroom.

Off: fans follow the normal Silent / Balanced / Turbo curve.
Felt a stutter? Press Ctrl+Alt+L in-game. D-Helper records the moment and, if the CPU was at its limit, reacts harder right away.

What D-Helper has learned

Every few minutes it compares the current power cap with 5 W more and 5 W less, and keeps whichever gives the fastest steady clock. Lag reports count against a cap. The best cap is remembered per game.
